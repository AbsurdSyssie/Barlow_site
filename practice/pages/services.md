## Clinics and services

The practice offers:

- Maternity services
- Cervical screening
- Child health and immunisations
- NHS Health Checks
- Asthma and COPD clinics
- Diabetes clinic
- Travel services
- Family planning
- Minor surgery
- Non-NHS services
- Health assessments for patients over 75

For appointments and enquiries about these services, [contact the practice](/appointments/). Ask reception about any charges for non-NHS services.
