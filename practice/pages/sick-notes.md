## Request a fit note

To request a sick note, also called a fit note, contact the practice through [PATCHS](https://patchs.ai/practice/barlowmedicalcentre).

If you cannot complete the online request, call [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;9000](tel:01614459000) or visit reception for help.

For information about when you need a fit note and self-certification, read [NHS guidance about fit notes](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/gps/getting-a-fit-note/).
