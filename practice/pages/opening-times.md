## Evening and weekend appointments

Pre-booked appointments are available through our Primary Care Network at Barlow Medical Centre or Burnage Health Centre. Ask reception to arrange an appointment. These are separate from reception opening hours.

## When we are closed

For urgent medical help when the practice is closed, use [NHS 111 online](https://111.nhs.uk/) or call [111](tel:111). In a life-threatening emergency, call [999](tel:999).
