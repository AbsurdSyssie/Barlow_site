## Check our practice area

We serve the M20 area of Manchester. Call [0161&nbsp;445&nbsp;9000](tel:01614459000) to check whether your address is within our practice boundary.

## How to register

Visit reception **between 10am and 6pm** to register. The admin team reviews registrations, which can take up to **2 working days**. Your NHS number is helpful but is not essential.

## Temporary residents

If you are visiting friends or relatives and need to register temporarily, speak to reception at the surgery.
