title: Register with the practice

This demonstration cannot register patients. Start from the [current NHS practice profile](https://www.nhs.uk/services/gp-surgery/barlow-medical-centre/P84034).

The archived registration page described an **M20 catchment area**, online registration, or in-person registration between **10am and 6pm**. It said the admin team would review registrations, taking up to 2 working days, and that an NHS number was helpful but not essential.

The archived catchment page used an interactive postcode tool. The actual boundary has not been recovered or confirmed. Do not assume that every M20 address is eligible. The archive directed eligibility questions to reception on **0161 445 9000**.

The archived page also described temporary resident registration at the surgery for visiting friends or relatives. Current eligibility and processes need confirmation.

The original registration destination is preserved in the migration evidence, not enabled as a demo form.
