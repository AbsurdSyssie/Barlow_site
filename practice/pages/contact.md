## Access and parking

The archived contact page lists step-free access, wheelchair access, an induction loop, a disabled WC and disabled parking. It lists car and cycle parking.

The archive warns of ANPR cameras and asks visitors to give their vehicle registration at reception to avoid a fine. Confirm current parking rules and access arrangements with the practice before travelling. No claim about free parking is made.
