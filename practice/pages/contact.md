## Contact us online

Use [PATCHS](https://patchs.ai/practice/barlowmedicalcentre) for medical and admin requests. See [appointments and home visits](/appointments/) for request times and help with booking.

If you cannot use the online service, call reception or visit the surgery for help.

### Parking

Car, cycle and disabled parking are available. ANPR cameras operate in the car park. Give your vehicle registration at reception to avoid a parking fine.

### Access and communication support

The surgery has step-free and wheelchair access, a disabled toilet and an induction loop.

Tell reception if you need an interpreter or help with access or communication.
